Professional Profile

Highly skilled researcher with expertise in ecological and evolutionary genomics, plant biology, climate change, and bioinformatics. Experienced in analyzing large-scale genomic data, conducting climate change adaptation studies, and teaching advanced scientific concepts. Adept at both independent and collaborative research in academic and non-academic settings.

Appointments

Purdue University, USA

Postdoctoral Research Associate, 2026–Present

  • Application of machine-learning approaches to investigate climate pre- or mal-adaptation for species with high restoration potential.
  • Identifying seed sources for regeneration planting in zones of mal-adaptation.

University of Vermont, USA

Graduate Research Assistant, 2018–2025

  • Investigated genotypic and phenotypic variation and climate change adaptation in red spruce.
  • Quantified biomass and trait variance using quantitative genetics and Bayesian approaches.
  • Investigated introgression and its role in local adaptation.
  • Used GWAS and bioinformatic methods to study adaptive trait architecture.

Graduate Teaching Assistant, 2018–2025

  • Taught Principles of Biology I and II, Exploring Biology, and Ecology and Evolution.
  • Trained students in genetics, wet lab methods, microscopy, protein purification, SDS-PAGE, and data analysis in R.

The Nature Conservancy, USA

Graduate Research Intern, 2021 & 2022

  • Applied genomic-assisted seed source selection for red spruce restoration.
  • Contributed to restoration efforts resulting in over 58,000 seedlings planted across three states.

Kerala Agricultural University, India

Graduate Teaching Assistant, 2014–2016

  • Supported teaching in biochemistry, biotechnology, tree improvement, tree physiology, world forest systems, and ecophysiology.

Graduate Research Assistant, 2011–2014

  • Investigated particulate pollution impacts on growth, physiology, and anatomy in teak and other deciduous tree species.

Independent Undergraduate Researcher, 2010–2011

  • Studied home garden structure and integrated farming system models.
